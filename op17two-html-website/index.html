Our Mission

Protecting the Innocent

Welcome to Operation 17:2, where every purchase and every donation helps us in our critical mission: to protect our children and get child predators off the street. Join our community as we stand together against child exploitation.

We're grateful for your support in building a safer world. Every decoy trained, every predator confronted, every team launched — none of it happens without people like you.
