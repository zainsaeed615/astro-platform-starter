About Us

Our Mission

A Christ-centered non-profit organization dedicated to protecting vulnerable children, defending those who cannot speak for themselves, and reflecting the justice, mercy, and truth of Jesus Christ through bold action, compassion, and unwavering biblical conviction.

Operation 17:2 exists because children deserve protection and predators deserve accountability. We are not vigilantes — we are a disciplined, Christ-centered organization that operates within the law, under leadership, and with prayer as our foundation.

Every purchase in our shop, every donation, and every volunteer hour directly supports our work: training decoys, documenting confrontations, coordinating with law enforcement, and expanding teams across the country.

"Better a millstone around his neck and cast into the sea than cause a little one to sin." — Luke 17:2

We reflect the justice, mercy, and truth of Jesus Christ through bold action. We refuse to look away while children are exploited. We stand together — Detect, Protect, Defend.
