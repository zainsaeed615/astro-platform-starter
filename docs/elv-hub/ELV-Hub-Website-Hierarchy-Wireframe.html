1. Document purpose & working method

This document answers the request raised in the client call: agree the skeleton before building the house. We are not proposing colours, banner sizes, final wording, or a live mock-up at this stage.

Agreed sequence of work
  1. Structure — hierarchy, sections, journey, CTAs (this document)
  2. Priorities — client selects section order and options
  3. Visual design — black/white layout concepts → then brand colours
  4. Content — final wording, photography / compressed hero video
  5. Development — build the approved structure into the live site

Note: The existing developed draft at elvhub.appointics.com remains a temporary reference only. It is not the approved structure. Layout decisions in that draft do not lock priorities.

6. Global navigation & header options

6.1 Recommended primary navigation

Remove “Home”. Do not lead with “About Us”. Prefer visitor tasks.

Proposed top navigation

Solutions (mega or simple dropdown) · Projects · About (optional in top nav or footer-only) · Contact Us (button-style CTA)

Search: removed. Phone/email/location utility bar: optional (see 6.3).

Nav itemLeads toNotes
Logo/Only “home” affordance needed
Solutions/solutions/ + dropdown to each solution pagePrimary discovery path
Projects/projects/Credibility path
About/about/Can live in footer only if client wants leaner nav
Contact Us/contact/Replaces “Request a Quote” as the single primary header action

6.2 Header behaviour (AVI-SPL inspired)

Recommended

Transparent over homepage hero → on scroll, solid/blurred bar. Inner pages: solid from load (no hero video). Nav labels sized to use available width after search removal. Slim height — no oversized white bar.

6.3 Top utility bar (phone / email / location)

Option U1 — Remove (recommended default)

Cleaner, modern. Contact details live in Contact Us, footer, and WhatsApp float. Matches client preference for non-legacy feel.

Option U2 — Keep slim utility bar

Helps older visitors find a phone number immediately without scrolling. Client said this remains undecided — keep as toggleable option.

7. Homepage section wireframes

Blocks below follow Option A order. Each block states: what it is, why it exists, and where CTAs go. No visual styling implied.

Global — Header (all pages)
HDR
Elements: Logo · Solutions · Projects · [About] · Contact Us button · [optional utility bar] · [optional WhatsApp icon]
Purpose: Persistent wayfinding + one clear conversion action.
CTAs: Logo → / · Solutions → /solutions/ · Projects → /projects/ · About → /about/ · Contact Us → /contact/
H1 — Hero
H1
Elements: Full-bleed media (compressed control-room / high-tech video or still) · Brand presence · One headline that states what ELV Hub does · One short supporting line · One primary CTA · Optional secondary CTA
Purpose: In one glance, visitor knows: Audiovisual + IT/ELV + Security · Design, supply, install · UAE / Middle East. Matches the clarity of the current elv-hub.com opening statement.
CTAs: Primary “Contact Us” → /contact/ · Secondary “Explore Solutions” → #solutions-overview or /solutions/
H2 — Trusted by / Client & partner logos
H2
Elements: Short label (“Trusted by”) · Logo strip (clients and/or manufacturer partners — client to choose mix)
Purpose: Instant credibility (SMERK pattern). No long text. First impression after hero.
CTAs: Optional: logos link to related project or stay non-clickable. “View projects” → /projects/
H3 — Solutions overview (icons)
H3
Elements: Section title · 4–5 icons with short labels only (AV · IT/ELV · Video Conferencing · Energy Saving · [Security])
Purpose: Fast mental model of offerings — the “old site icons” lesson. Less text, faster recognition even for non-technical visitors.
CTAs: Each icon → matching /solutions/[slug]/
H4 — Solutions in depth (image rows / cards)
H4
Elements: For each solution: image · title · 1–2 line description · “Read more”
Purpose: After the overview, give a slightly deeper picture without dumping full capability lists on the homepage. Detail lives on solution pages.
CTAs: Read more → /solutions/[slug]/ Section end link “All solutions” → /solutions/
H5 — How we work (4 steps)
H5
Elements: 1 Consultancy & Design · 2 Project Management · 3 Implementation · 4 Annual Maintenance / Support
Purpose: Show end-to-end accountability (AVI-SPL Design/Build/Manage pattern, adapted to ELV Hub’s service list). Builds trust before project stories.
CTAs: Optional “Talk to a specialist” → /contact/
H6 — Selected projects
H6
Elements: 3–5 featured projects (image, name, sector tag, one-line outcome)
Purpose: Proof. Landmark names (e.g. MBRSC, Huawei, Wego, Habtoor) do the selling.
CTAs: Project card → /projects/[slug]/ “View all projects” → /projects/
H7 — Sectors we serve
H7
Elements: Education · Hospitality · Oil & Gas · Corporate · Government (icons or simple labels)
Purpose: Help visitor self-identify. Optional later: each sector links to filtered projects.
CTAs: Sector → /projects/?sector=… (if filters exist) or stay informational in Phase 1
H8 — Why ELV Hub (short)
H8
Elements: 3–4 points max (certifications BICSI / AVIXA / USGBC · manufacturer partnerships · end-to-end delivery · UAE track record)
Purpose: Reinforce trust without turning homepage into an About page.
CTAs: “About the company” → /about/
H9 — Final CTA band
H9
Elements: Short invitation · Contact Us · Phone · WhatsApp
Purpose: Capture the inquiry after the journey is complete.
CTAs: Contact Us → /contact/ · tel: link · WhatsApp deep link
Global — Footer (all pages)
FTR
Elements: Logo · short positioning line · Solutions links · Projects · About · Contact · Address · Phone · Email · Social · Legal
Purpose: Secondary navigation + contact details always available.
CTAs: Same destinations as nav; email mailto:; phone tel:

Removed from homepage vs current draft (recommended): large FAQ block (move to Contact or Phase 2), heavy “stats counters” unless real numbers are confirmed, duplicate contact forms (keep one primary form on Contact page; homepage can deep-link).

10. Design-system notes deferred (recorded, not decided here)

These were raised in the meeting but intentionally belong to the visual design phase, after structure approval:

  • Reduce pure-white section backgrounds; prefer soft light-blue tones aligned with logo.
  • Keep professional blue gradient treatments where they already feel strong.
  • Transparent sticky header; reduce header height; enlarge nav label weight/size.
  • Avoid all-caps overload in headings (content/typography rule later).
  • Hero: short compressed high-tech / control-room video; replaceable mock footage OK.
  • Mobile experience: same journey, stacked sections, large tap targets on Contact.
