Working With Us

Employment FAQ

Where do I apply?
Click the link in the Employment tab that says Apply Now! When we receive your inquiry we will send a follow-up application.
What are the hours?
Ichabod Payne's Sleepy Hollow applicants must be available every Saturday and Sunday from September 19th to October 31st. Sleepy Hollow will not be open on Sunday, November 1st. Park hours are 11am - 5 pm. Shifts will start 30 minutes to 1 hour before opening and end 30 minutes to 1 hour past closing. Payne's Labyrinth applicants must be available every Friday through Sunday from September 19th to November 1st. Park hours begin at 6 and end times vary based on weekend. Shifts will start 30 minutes to 1 hour before opening and end 30 minutes to 1 hour past closing.
What are the pay rates?
Pay starts at $10 per hour, dependent on position.
Is there on-site lodging for employees?
No. We do not offer on‑site lodging for employees.
How old do I have to be?
You must be 16 years of age to work at Ichabod Payne's Sleepy Hollow or Payne's Labyrinth.
What type of positions are available?
We have a wide variety of needs for our team, including scare and character actors, customer service, retail, concessions, parking attendant, janitorial, and security positions.
Do I have to provide my own uniform?
Payne's Labyrinth Scare actors/characters: Yes — you must provide your own costume, makeup, and accessories. Ichabod Payne's Sleepy Hollow: Employees must be modest, clean, and non‑frightening in appearance. Colored hair, piercings, & tattoos are allowed as long as they are not offensive, frightening, or depict illegal substances. Customer service, retail, and support roles: You may provide your own uniform, or you may receive one based on the current season's theme.
If I am interested in being a character, do I need to audition?
Yes. We will have open casting calls and dates will be announced under our employment tab. Virtual auditions will be scheduled upon request, but in-person is always preferred.
What happens during a Payne's Labyrinth audition?
Rather than a traditional audition with a reading or monologue, you may be asked to demonstrate scary behavior and project your voice or scream. Having a character prepared is not required, but if you do have an original character, we would love to see it!
Do I pick my own character?
Generally, no. Management assigns roles based on audition strengths and production needs. Original characters: You may present your own concept; if it fits a future event, you may be asked to use it then.
Can customers touch me?
ABSOLUTELY NOT. We have a strict harassment policy for our staff & patrons. If at any time a patron purposely touches IMMEDIATELY contact security so they can be removed.
Can I touch customers?
In general, NO. However, there are two designated physical contact nights. On those nights, patrons who have signed a waiver agreeing to physical contact will be clearly & plainly marked. DO NOT touch anyone who is not wearing the designated physical contact marker, even if it is during the physical touch time period. There will be zero tolerance for an actor purposefully physically touching any patron at any time that is NOT wearing the marker.
Do I need transportation to and from the attractions?
Yes. Team members are responsible for their own transportation to and from the job site.
Are there any physical limitations to any position?
Ichabod Payne's Sleepy Hollow has positions available for all ages (16 and up) and physical abilities! Payne's Labyrinth: While only a small number of roles can accommodate certain physical limitations, we encourage you to apply — we're always happy to explore the best fit for your abilities. Working in a haunted attraction can be physically demanding. Depending on the role, employees may be required to: • Stand for extended periods (often 3–6 hours at a time) • Walk or move continuously through dimly lit or uneven areas • Perform repetitive motions, including reaching, bending, crouching, or quick directional changes • Work in low‑light, fog, strobe, or loud environments safely and without discomfort • Tolerate heat, cold, or outdoor weather conditions • Lift or carry lightweight props (typically under 20 lbs) • Navigate tight spaces or narrow corridors • Maintain vocal stamina if the role involves speaking, shouting, or character interaction • Wear costumes, masks, makeup, or themed attire for extended periods • Remain alert and responsive to guests, supervisors, and safety protocols at all times
Will you run a background check?
Yes. Due to the presence of minors, insurance regulations prohibit us from hiring anyone with a felony charge or conviction, or a record of any sex crimes, crimes against a minor, or fraud.
Can I work at both attractions?
Yes! However, your position may vary depending on which concept you are scheduled to work.
Will I be drug tested?
No. However, due to the presence of minors, we have a ZERO TOLERANCE policy for intoxication in the workplace. Any employee who arrives or is caught under the influence of alcohol, illicit or illegal drugs, or any other substance that causes impairment will be IMMEDIATELY terminated and removed from the attractions.
