Bad Weather Policy

In the event of inclement weather during your visit, attractions will not continue to operate. For the safety and comfort of our Guests and Cast, Attractions may close until the inclement weather passes.

Please visit the check in station during your visit for updated information. In the event the weather gets too bad, all guests will be asked to leave the park for your safety.

If the park closes 2 or more hours early, you will be eligible to return on another open night this season. When exiting the park, present your ticket to the staff in the check in station to be placed on the bad weather guest list.

We will keep our social media updated with relevant weather information. We recommend you check these notices before scheduling your visit.
