Subscription and Cancellation Policy

Mindfulness Monthly and Premium Mindfulness Monthly are recurring subscription programs.

By enrolling, you authorize Mindfulness Candle Collection to charge your selected payment method automatically at the beginning of each billing period until your subscription is canceled.

You may cancel your subscription before the next scheduled billing date by accessing your customer account or contacting our customer-care team at info@mindfulnesscandle.store.

Cancellation requests received after an order has been processed may apply to the following billing period. Orders that have already entered fulfillment cannot ordinarily be canceled.

Subscription products, fragrances, and benefits may vary based on availability. Any material changes to pricing or subscription terms will be communicated before they take effect.

For assistance, contact us at info@mindfulnesscandle.store.
