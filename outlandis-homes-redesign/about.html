Our People

Meet the Crew

OL

Oliver Landis III

Founder & Owner

Oliver started Outlandis in 2011 after years in corporate America, building a family-operated company rooted in faith, family, and hard work. A proud Marine Corps veteran who served during the Gulf War, he owns and manages two communities in the Columbia area with nearly 150 pads. His mission: help as many people as possible become homeowners.

OL

Olivia Landis

Partner

Olivia graduated from the University of South Carolina with degrees in Biology and Psychology before a career in Occupational Therapy. She combines her background with the family's mission, guiding clients through the home-buying journey with care, patience, and attention to detail.

GR

Griffin Reese

Partner

Griffin studied Historic Preservation at the College of Charleston and gained experience in commercial brokerage and development. Today he works alongside a team that believes in going the extra mile, staying hands-on, and making deals work in ways larger sellers often won't.
