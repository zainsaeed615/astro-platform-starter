Our Story

More Than a Candle. A Moment Created for You.

Life can be demanding. Between professional responsibilities, caregiving, health challenges, grief, family obligations, and everyday uncertainty, we often forget to pause and care for ourselves.

Mindfulness Candle Collection creates faith-centered wellness experiences that invite you to slow down, breathe deeply, reflect intentionally, and reconnect with what matters most.

From our clean-burning soy candles and flame-free fragrance products to our journals, affirmation cards, comforting blankets, and curated wellness bundles, every item is designed to help transform ordinary moments into meaningful rituals of peace, prayer, restoration, and renewal.

Community Impact

Wellness That Creates Community Impact

Every qualifying purchase helps extend the impact of mindfulness beyond the individual.

Mindfulness Candle Collection contributes 10% of qualifying product sales to Friendship That Gives, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization advancing health education, patient advocacy, caregiver wellness, and community empowerment.

Through this partnership, your purchase helps support historically underserved individuals and families affected by chronic illnesses, rare diseases, caregiving responsibilities, mental-health challenges, and barriers to equitable care.

When you choose Mindfulness Candle Collection, you are not simply purchasing a product. You are helping create moments of restoration while supporting a broader movement of education, advocacy, hope, and healing.
