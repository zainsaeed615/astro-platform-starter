# Quality Assurance

## 1) Quality Management Philosophy
The factory follows a professional quality management approach with full-process control, supported by:
- Professional quality management teams
- Total Quality Management (TQM) principles
- Standardized documentation and record traceability
- Technical after-sales support systems

The operation is aligned with internationally recognized systems, including **ISO 9001** and **ISO 14001**.

## 2) Compliance and Environmental Responsibility
Material and product compliance follows major international requirements:
- **RoHS** compliance
- **REACH** compliance
- Hazardous substance control using **XRF testing**

This compliance framework helps customers reduce regulatory risk when entering international markets.

## 3) Full-Process Quality Control
Quality control spans the complete product lifecycle:
1. Incoming material inspection
2. Process and in-line production control
3. Final product quality assurance
4. Customer follow-up and feedback closure

Production records are strictly controlled to ensure each stage is accurately documented and auditable.

## 4) Product Verification and Reliability Testing
The factory uses structured verification plans and targeted reliability testing, including:
- Reliability tests
- Environmental tests
- Structural tests
- Lifetime tests
- Dimensional measurement
- Physical characterization tests
- Function reliability tests
- Emission ingredient-related checks

The R&D and QA teams jointly identify and close design and process risks through checklist-driven reviews.

## 5) Testing Equipment and Lab Capability
Quality assurance is supported by specialized instrumentation, including:
- Analog battery test systems
- Oscilloscopes
- DC power supplies
- Manometers
- Quadratic image testers
- E-cigarette resistance testers
- CCD systems
- Smoke suction testers
- Programmable temperature and humidity chambers
- High-altitude low-pressure simulation chambers
- Transportation vibration testing machines
- Ceramic core porosity test machines
- Electronic load meters
- High-accuracy battery testing systems

This breadth of equipment enables data-backed validation from raw materials to finished products.

## 6) Risk Prevention in New Product Development
During new product development, cross-functional teams perform:
- Risk identification during design phase
- Structural/electronic/ID reviews
- Targeted test planning by project
- Progressive problem closure before launch

This method reduces field failure risk and strengthens first-pass quality at production ramp-up.

## 7) Added Value: Quality Assurance as a Business Advantage
Quality assurance in this factory is not only an inspection function; it is a competitive system that:
1. Improves brand reliability and customer trust
2. Lowers warranty, return, and rework costs
3. Increases speed-to-market by reducing late-stage defects
4. Supports stable long-term OEM/ODM partnerships

By combining standards, process control, and verification depth, the factory delivers both regulatory confidence and product consistency.
