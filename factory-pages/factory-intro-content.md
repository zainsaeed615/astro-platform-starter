# Factory Intro

## 1) Company Overview
Dongguan Weisi Electronic Technology Co., Ltd. was founded in January 2019 and has continuously scaled its operations year by year. The company operates as a professional e-cigarette manufacturer integrating R&D, production, sales, and service.

## 2) Core Business and Product Scope
Main product categories include:
- Disposable electronic cigarettes
- Disposable rechargeable electronic cigarettes
- Closed-cartridge pod systems
- Open-cartridge pod systems
- Power boxes
- Disposable CBD devices
- Open large-smoke atomizers

The product portfolio supports both fast-moving consumer demand and OEM/ODM collaboration models.

## 3) Factory Scale and Capacity
- **Dust-free production standard:** 100,000-class clean plant
- **Production area:** 3,000 square meters
- **Total facility area:** 5,000 square meters
- **Employees:** 100-200
- **Current annual output:** 5,000,000 pieces
- **Annual capacity:** 10,000,000 pieces

This scale allows flexible response to pilot runs, medium-volume launches, and high-volume commercial production.

## 4) Organization and Operational Structure
The organizational structure is built around cross-functional execution:
- General Manager and Director leadership
- Sales Department
- R&D Department and project management
- Product Department
- Finance
- Plant Manager
- Manufacturing and Production Engineering
- PMC and Purchasing
- Quality Center (incoming, process, and finished goods assurance)
- HR

This structure supports rapid decision-making, traceable ownership, and coordinated execution from concept to shipment.

## 5) Team Strength and Technical Foundation
All key R&D personnel have over 10 years of product design and development experience in the e-cigarette field. Leadership coverage includes manufacturing, product, R&D, quality, engineering, and supply chain directors.

Engineering and design capabilities are supported by tools such as:
- CAD
- Altium Designer
- Jialitron EDA
- PADS
- Huaqiu DFM

These tools help optimize product design for manufacturability, reliability, and market competitiveness.

## 6) Supply Chain and Data-Driven Operations
The factory applies professional supplier qualification and elimination mechanisms, with cross-functional assessments across R&D, quality, procurement, and finance. It also uses ERP-based data management to connect CRM, order flow, planning, purchasing, shipment, and accounting processes.

This creates stronger delivery predictability, cost visibility, and process compliance.

## 7) Added Value: Why This Factory Is a Reliable Long-Term Partner
Beyond production capacity, the factory demonstrates three long-term partnership advantages:
1. **Integrated model:** End-to-end control from product definition to after-sales support.
2. **Scalable execution:** Ability to support both market testing and rapid scale-up.
3. **Continuous improvement culture:** Strong focus on optimization in design, process, and supply chain performance.

Together, these strengths reduce project risk, shorten commercialization cycles, and improve consistency for global customers.
