Manufacturing Excellence

Quality Assurance

Quality management is implemented as a full-process system, not only final inspection. The factory applies Total Quality Management practices, documented controls, and cross-functional quality ownership to ensure stable product performance and global compliance.

Standards

ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 aligned management and audit-ready documentation.

Compliance

RoHS and REACH implementation with hazardous substance checks by XRF.

Verification

Environmental, reliability, structural, dimensional, and life-cycle tests.

After-Sales

Technical service support to close quality feedback loops quickly.

End-to-End Quality Control Flow

  1. 1. Incoming Quality Control: Strict inspection and qualification of materials before line release.
  2. 2. In-Process Control: Process checkpoints with standard records and issue traceability.
  3. 3. Final Quality Assurance: Finished goods verification before shipment.
  4. 4. Customer Follow-Up: Service and feedback closure for continual improvement.

Added Value: QA as a Growth Engine

The factory integrates QA into design, sourcing, and manufacturing decisions. This reduces launch risk, improves first-pass yield, lowers after-sales cost, and strengthens confidence for long-term OEM/ODM partnerships in global markets.
