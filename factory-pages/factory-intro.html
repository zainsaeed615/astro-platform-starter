Factory Profile

Factory Intro

Dongguan Weisi Electronic Technology Co., Ltd., founded in January 2019, is an integrated manufacturer combining R&D, production, sales, and service for atomization and e-cigarette products. The company has expanded year by year with a modern plant environment and a scalable production model.

Core Product Portfolio

  • • Disposable electronic cigarettes
  • • Disposable rechargeable electronic cigarettes
  • • Closed-cartridge pod systems
  • • Open-cartridge pod systems
  • • Power boxes and large-smoke atomizers
  • • Disposable CBD equipment

Organization and Execution Model

The management framework connects leadership, sales, R&D, project management, manufacturing, engineering, PMC, purchasing, quality assurance, finance, and HR into one coordinated operating system. This cross-functional structure improves speed, ownership, and delivery reliability from concept to shipment.

R&D and Technical Strength

Key R&D members bring 10+ years of industry experience. Design and engineering workflows are supported by CAD, Altium Designer, Jialitron EDA, PADS, and Huaqiu DFM to optimize functionality, manufacturability, and product consistency.
